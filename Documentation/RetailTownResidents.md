# Retail town residents (field sprites recovered from TOWN.BIN)

Generated by `Tools/extract_retail_town_residents.py` (validated byte-identical
against the installed banks). Installed in
`Assets/AzureDreams/Resources/Town/Residents/Retail/<bank>.png|.json` and
played by `RetailTownResidentSpriteView` (same format as Vivian).

## How a person is defined

* 115 initializers in TOWN.BIN call `register_person` (`jal 0x800C3050`):
  `a1` = person slot, `a2`/`a3` = private tables, `sp+16` = idle/walk clip
  tables, `sp+20` = action clip tables.
* Slot = `F_act_<name>` − 62 (`script_symbols.h`). Confirmed visually
  (Barry 16, Cherrl 13, Vivian 12, cat 68, dancers 70–73, horses 75–78 without
  sprite clips) and by the RainCast prologue cels matching `gigi`/`obasan`.
* Many residents share one body; the initializer then stores
  `SpriteFrameState.clut` (+0x12), which `sprite_projection.c` adds to every
  command CLUT. That is how Hush (+576) differs from Tonka, Silver (+576) from
  Dar (+704) and Romi (+0), and Dr. Hal (+640) from Grandpa (+0).
* Textures/palettes: TownCharacterPackages `resource_06` (rows 256–383 and all
  person CLUTs) + `resource_01` (rows 384–511); Guy needs `resource_03`.

## Scene speaker tables (speaker id = person slot)

Scene overlays keep a table of `(u16 speaker, u16 index, u32 script)`; the
speaker id equals the person slot. Found: gym 0x47159C, monster shop
0x601750, racetrack lobby 0x4D1104 (bet, kicca, silver, dai, setumeiin, horses),
casino roulette 0x451C8C (setumeiin, dai, der, bet), slots 0x4598D8 (kicca,
bany, romi, vivian), hospital 0x5976F4 (hush, cherrl, guy), Koh family house
0x65A168 (obasan, neko, gigi, ozisan), Nico house 0x65699C (niko, nikotiti),
Cherrl house 0x647DFC (cherrl, nerdia), prologue 0x3F55E0 (player, rees, wedy,
niko, yoda).

Validation against the scene-specific casino/gym atlases (extracted
separately by extract_minigame_residents.py): gambler = bet, broke = dai,
woman = kicca, old man = romi, Kim = kimani all match their speaker slot.
The atlases named RouletteAttendant and CasinoLoser match slots 44 and 43
respectively, i.e. their names were swapped; `TowerGameController.Casino.cs`
now applies them to the right residents.

Residents present in the original scenes but absent in Unity: the uncle
(ozisan) in Koh's family house and Cherrl's mother (nerdia, Cherrl house).

Unity ids without a proven slot (chero, monster_shop_hunter,
restaurant_hunter, arcade_attendant, theater_audience, pool_visitor) keep their
current visuals. Bany (51/81) needs a scene package not decoded here. The block of
initializers at file offset 0x516xx+ (palettes 0x77xx) is the pool/swimsuit
variant set and needs scene packages too.

## Pool scene (overlay 0x51E000)

Speaker table 0x520D34 lists the heroines, Ghosh, aniki/otouto, slots 83/84,
kiku, miru, gigi (grandpa), narder, hunter1, obasan, oyaji, baba and
**Barry with the two Kim brothers** (slots 16, 41, 42 share the script at
0x8001AEF0). Their swimsuit bodies are the initializers at 0x51690+ (CLUTs
0x77xx), which need the pool scene package; Unity's pool currently has none
of these residents.

### Pool residents implemented (2026-11-18)

* Swimsuit package: TOWN.BIN 0x8B8000 (rows 384-511, palettes 0x7700); with
  resource_06 it renders the pool initializers 0x51690-0x51F08 correctly.
  `extract_retail_town_residents.py` now decodes it and writes `pool_<name>` banks
  (aniki, baba, bary, gigi, gosh, hunter1, kiku, kimani, kimotouto, miru, obasan,
  otouto, oyaji, slot83, slot84).
* Text: Shift-JIS in the overlay; script VA -> file = VA - 0x80010000 + 0x5298D0;
  the bytecode switches speaker with 0x57 <slot>. Lines per resident are in
  `TowerGameController.PoolResidents.cs` with their offsets.
* Barry + Kims share one script; the two answers' replies ("You're so mean" vs. the
  three "Eeeek!") were assigned by meaning, not by decoding the branch opcodes.
* aniki/otouto (Vivian-date hecklers) were extracted but are not spawned: they
  belong to the Vivian pool event.
* Positions are free cells (native OSETDT positions not decoded).
