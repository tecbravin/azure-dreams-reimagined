# Azure Dreams Reimagined — Asset Manifest

Generated by Art Director. Assets below distinguish verified disc data from reconstructed Unity content and unclassified previews.

## Graphics

### Town VRAM Uploads (Decoded Bytes, Visual Mapping Pending)
- Twelve raw 32-word ×128-row uploads and 17 palette records are extracted from `TOWN.BIN+0x72D800` under `../extracted_assets/verified_decode/art_validation_20261102/`.
- The existing `Player_Albedo.png`, `Townsperson_Albedo.png` and `Familiar_Albedo.png` are byte-for-byte legacy RGB555 previews of uploads 0, 9 and 6. The audit found that this conversion does not establish the game's bpp, STP/transparency handling, or semantic binding to those characters.
- Do not use these PNGs as final portraits or albedo textures until their consumers' TPAGE/CLUT mappings are resolved. Town NPC prefabs now use authored flat palette materials and leave portrait references empty.
- Evidence and decode limits: `../extracted_assets/verified_decode/art_validation_20261102/ui_vram_evidence.md` and `manifest.json`.

### Town Terrain, Buildings & NPC Prefabs (Integrated, Provisional Reconstruction)
| Asset | Location | Source / fidelity |
|---|---|---|
| Grass, path, plaster, roof, wood, stone and water pixel textures | `Assets/AzureDreams/Textures/TownGenerated/` | Authored 32×32 palette textures; point filtered, lossless, no mipmaps. These are visual reconstructions, not decoded retail texture pages. |
| Town ground, path tile, fence, tower gate, fountain, guild sign and chest | `Assets/AzureDreams/Resources/Town/` | Reusable low-poly prefabs loaded by the runtime Town generator. |
| Inn, Market and Residence | `Assets/AzureDreams/Resources/Town/House_*.prefab` | Reconstructed building geometry; original TOWN.BIN building meshes and texture descriptors remain undecoded. |
| Mira and Rowan | `Assets/AzureDreams/Resources/Town/NPC_*.prefab` | Reconstructed low-poly character meshes with provisional palette colors. The raw Town VRAM preview is deliberately unassigned; original character models, animation clips and exact texture/CLUT bindings remain unresolved. |

The runtime Town now loads these prefabs and a connected tiled path between spawn, houses, fountain, guild board and tower gate. Returning from the tower respawns the player in front of the reconstructed gate, with facing restored for re-entry. The decomp confirms door transitions use destination position records (`town/map/door.c`), but the exact retail Town return coordinates have not yet been resolved from `TOWN.BIN`; the Unity return pose therefore remains provisional. `TownAssetPrefabBuilder` can rebuild or verify the generated assets from the Azure Dreams menu.

Town script, shop and placement evidence plus unresolved retail-data bindings are tracked in `TOWN_PORT_STATUS.md`.
